Somewhere between November and March, a warm week stops being a luxury and starts being a sanity measure. The trick is knowing where a European family can actually rely on the sun in winter, because plenty of "sunny" destinations are a gamble by December. Here are eight resorts where the warmth is close to guaranteed for 2027, scored for families and mapped so you can compare dates.

## Where winter sun is genuinely reliable

- **The Canary Islands** are the obvious answer: 20 to 22 degrees all winter, a four-hour flight, and Spain-standard family resorts. Gran Canaria, Fuerteventura, Tenerife and Lanzarote are the anchors.
- **The Red Sea and the Gulf** turn it up a notch in heat, if you are happy with a longer flight for near-certain sun.
- **Cape Verde** sits in between, reliably warm and low-key.

## What to check for a winter trip

- **A heated pool.** Winter air is cooler even where the sea is warm, so an unheated pool can sit unused all week.
- **Indoor and covered options** for the odd grey afternoon.
- **A short transfer**, which matters more with tired children and shorter daylight.
