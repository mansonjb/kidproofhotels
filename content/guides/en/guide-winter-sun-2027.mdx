Somewhere past November a warm week stops being a treat and becomes the thing that keeps a family sane. For 2027 the reliable answers from western Europe are, in order of flight time, the Canary Islands (about four hours, warm all winter), Cape Verde (five to six hours), Morocco's Agadir, and then the long-haul heat of the Red Sea, Dubai and Ras Al Khaimah, or Mauritius. With a single week of holiday you do not gamble on the weather, so this guide is about where the sun is genuinely dependable from December to March.

## Where can a European family find real winter sun?

The Canaries are the simplest answer and we recommend them without a second thought: a four-hour flight, around 20 to 22 degrees in January and February, calm bays where little ones keep their footing, and hotels built for families. Gran Canaria, Fuerteventura, Lanzarote and Tenerife all work. Beyond them, Cape Verde (the islands of Sal and Boa Vista) is a warmer, quieter step further out. For real heat, the kind that makes you sweat in December, the Red Sea (Sharm el Sheikh, Hurghada), Dubai and Ras Al Khaimah, and Mauritius push the thermometer higher, at the cost of a longer flight.

## Canaries or the Red Sea and Gulf?

| | Canary Islands | Red Sea / Dubai |
|---|---|---|
| Flight from western Europe | about 4 hours | 5 to 7 hours |
| Winter temperature | 20 to 22C | 24 to 28C |
| Sea | calm Atlantic bays | very warm, snorkelling |
| Best for | short haul, young kids | guaranteed heat, older kids |

Short version: the Canaries win on flight time and are the softer landing with a toddler, while the Red Sea and the Gulf win on sheer warmth and are better once children handle a longer flight. Cape Verde sits neatly between the two.

## How warm is it really, month by month?

December to February is the coolest stretch. The Canaries hold roughly 20 to 22 degrees by day with a cooler Atlantic sea, so a heated pool matters. Cape Verde runs a little warmer at 24 to 26. The Red Sea and the Gulf sit around 24 to 28 by day with warm water, the reason people fly the extra hours. Everywhere, evenings cool down in winter, so pack a fleece for the little ones and do not expect to swim in the sea unheated in the Canaries.

## Why does a short transfer matter so much?

Put this first when you choose. After an early flight with a wiped-out toddler, an extra hour on a coach is the difference between a good first evening and a full meltdown. Airports like Gran Canaria, Fuerteventura and Sharm el Sheikh sit close to their main resort strips, so you can often be poolside within 30 to 45 minutes. Check the transfer time before the price.

## What should we budget?

Think in bands. The Canaries offer everything from budget self-catering apartments to mid-range family all-inclusives, and winter is not their most expensive season outside Christmas and the February half-term. Cape Verde and Morocco sit at the mid-range. Dubai, Ras Al Khaimah and Mauritius are the high-end tier once you add the longer flight. All-inclusive tends to pay off in the Red Sea and Gulf, where stepping outside the resort for meals is less practical, while in the Canaries half-board or self-catering can be better value.

## FAQ

**Where is the warmest short-haul winter sun from Europe?**
The Canary Islands, at around 20 to 22 degrees from December to March, roughly four hours' flight. For hotter still you go long-haul to the Red Sea, Dubai or Mauritius.

**Is the sea warm enough to swim in the Canaries in winter?**
The Atlantic is cool in winter, so choose a resort with a heated pool. The sea is fine for paddling on mild days but most families swim in the pool.

**Canaries or Cape Verde for a family?**
The Canaries for the shorter flight and the widest choice of family hotels; Cape Verde for slightly warmer weather and a quieter feel, at an extra hour or two in the air.

**When should we book winter sun for 2027?**
As soon as your dates are set, especially for Christmas, New Year and February half-term, when warm-destination capacity sells out months ahead.

**Is Dubai good for families in winter?**
Yes. Winter is Dubai's best season at 24 to 28 degrees, with big family resorts and water parks, though the six to seven hour flight suits older children better than babies.
