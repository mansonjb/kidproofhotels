Somewhere past November, a warm week stops being a treat and starts being the thing that keeps everyone sane instead of climbing the walls at home. The question is where, because plenty of destinations sold as sunny are a gamble by December. And in the depth of winter, with a single week to spend, you do not gamble on the weather.

The Canary Islands are the simplest answer: 20 to 22 degrees all winter, a four-hour flight, and family resorts built to Spanish standards. Gran Canaria, Fuerteventura, Tenerife, Lanzarote, you can hardly go wrong. To push the thermometer higher, the Red Sea and the Gulf deliver, at the cost of a longer flight. Cape Verde sits in between: warm, low-key, no fuss.

The detail everyone forgets is the heated pool. Winter air stays cool even where the sea is warm, and an unheated pool can sit empty all week. Check too that there is something to do indoors for the grey afternoon that always turns up eventually, and a short transfer, which matters more than you would think when night falls at six with tired children in tow.
