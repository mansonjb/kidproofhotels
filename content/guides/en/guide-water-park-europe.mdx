There are two camps. Those for whom the pool is a bonus, and those for whom the water park IS the holiday. If you are in the second, you already know that a proper aquapark, slides that hold up all day, a lazy river, a splash zone for the little ones, can keep children busy from morning to night. And change the whole equation of a week away.

You do need a real park, though, not a slide for the photo. What we look for: a shallow, shaded zone for the under-fives, kept apart from the big slides; enough to entertain a six year old and a twelve year old alike; and above all an aquapark that comes with the stay, not billed at the gate once you arrive. The detail that changes everything is being able to keep an eye on everyone from a lounger.

The Canaries and the Spanish costas build the biggest resort aquaparks, but Greece, Cyprus and the Adriatic make a strong case too. All eight below have a park worth travelling for, plus the rooms and the club to see the week through.
