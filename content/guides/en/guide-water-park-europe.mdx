For some families the water park is the holiday. A resort with a proper aquapark, real slides, a lazy river, a splash zone for toddlers, can keep children happy from breakfast to sundown, which changes the whole economics of a week away. Here are the European resorts where the water park is big enough to be the reason you book, scored for families and mapped for 2027.

## What separates a real water park from a token slide

- **A splash zone for under-fives**, shallow and shaded, separate from the big slides.
- **Height ranges covered**, so a six year old and a twelve year old both have something to ride.
- **Included, not a paid extra.** The best resorts fold the aquapark into the price; some charge for it once you arrive, so check.
- **Lifeguards and sightlines**, because a water park you cannot supervise from a lounger is a stressful one.

## Where to look in Europe

The Canary Islands and the Spanish costas build some of the biggest resort aquaparks, and Greece, Cyprus and the Adriatic have strong contenders too. All eight below have a water park worth travelling for, plus the family rooms and kids club to back it up.
