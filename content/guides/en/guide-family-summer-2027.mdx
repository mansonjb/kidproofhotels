Peak summer is when the Mediterranean does exactly what a family holiday needs: warm sea, long days, and resorts running at full tilt with the kids club, the pools and the evening entertainment all firing. The only hard part is choosing, and booking before the good weeks sell out. Here are the eight resorts we would book for summer 2027, scored and mapped.

## What makes a summer resort worth it

- **A free kids club that actually runs all day**, not a token session, because this is the week you want a couple of hours to yourself.
- **Pools built for the water-slide age**, plus a calm, shallow beach for the little ones.
- **An early dinner sitting and food children eat**, so evenings stay easy in the heat.
- **Rooms that fit the family**, with a separate sleeping space worth paying up for.

## Where to look

Greece leads for scored family resorts, with Spain and Italy close behind. Match the destination to your children: shallow bays and gentle resorts for toddlers, big water parks and more to do for older kids and teens.
