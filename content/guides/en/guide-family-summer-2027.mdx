Peak summer is the Mediterranean doing exactly what you ask of it: warm sea, long days, and resorts at full tilt, the club, the pools, the evening show, all running. The only real headache is choosing. And booking before the good weeks evaporate.

What makes a summer resort worth it? A free kids club that genuinely runs all day, not a token hour, because this is the week you dream of two hours to yourself. Pools built for the water-slide age, and a gentle beach for the little ones. An early dinner and food the children eat, so evenings stay light in the heat. And rooms that fit, with a separate sleeping space where you can get one.

Greece leads for scored family resorts, with Spain and Italy close behind. Match the destination to the children: shallow bays and gentle resorts for toddlers, big water parks and more to do for the older ones.
