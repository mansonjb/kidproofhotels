Cheap all-inclusive has a reputation to shake off: the sad buffet, the crowded pool. It is not always deserved. There are affordable places that nail the basics, the free club, the pool, food the kids eat without a fight, and others that cut exactly the corner that ruins the week. Telling them apart is the whole job.

What you want at a lower price is simple. A free kids club and a real pool, the first two things to vanish at the bottom of the market. A buffet that serves early and offers more than chips, so you are not topping up at the local shop. Family rooms that sleep four without turning bedtime into Tetris. And drinks and snacks genuinely included, not a list of three things with everything else charged extra.

The best value sits on the Spanish costas, the Canaries and the Greek islands, where supply runs deep and competition keeps prices down. Turkey often undercuts the lot, if a slightly longer flight does not put you off.
