Spain is the dependable engine of the family beach holiday, the one you come back to when you want sun without any nasty surprises. Short flights, a coast and islands that never seem to end, and always a resort at your price. The real strength is the flexibility: whatever the season and the ages, there is a good answer.

The Canary Islands first, because they run all year: Tenerife, Fuerteventura, Gran Canaria and Lanzarote are as much the February answer as the summer one. On the mainland and the Balearics, the gentle beaches of the southern costas keep the paddlers happy. And supply runs so deep that competition keeps prices and facilities honest.

The move is to match the beach to the age. Shallow and sandy for toddlers, with more to do nearby for teenagers, who lose patience by a pool fast.
