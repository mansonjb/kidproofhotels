Spain is the reliable engine of the European family beach holiday: sun you can count on, short flights, and a coast-and-island spread that means there is always a resort at your price. From the year-round Canaries to the mainland costas, here are eight Spanish beach resorts that genuinely work with children, scored and mapped for 2027.

## What makes Spain work with kids

- **The Canary Islands run all year**, so Tenerife, Fuerteventura, Gran Canaria and Lanzarote are the winter and half-term answer as much as the summer one.
- **Shallow, gentle beaches** on the Balearics and the southern costas for paddling ages.
- **Depth of supply**, which keeps family resorts competitive on price and facilities.
- **Everything from big water-park all-inclusives to calm aparthotels**, so the week fits your children, not the other way around.

## Where to base yourself

For guaranteed winter sun, the Canaries win. For a short summer hop, the Balearics and the Costa del Sol are hard to beat. Match the beach to the age: shallow and sandy for toddlers, more to do for teens.
