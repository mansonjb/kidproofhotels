Antalya makes a formidable family all-inclusive, no one will argue otherwise. But plenty of families would rather stay inside the EU: a shorter flight, no currency to change, the same rules, and sometimes simply a pull towards Greece or Spain. The good news is you give up nothing.

Greece has caught up and overtaken, the Ikos resorts leading, with a la carte dining included and proper kids clubs. Spain brings the consistency, from the Canaries to the costas, with well-drilled resorts and a short flight. Italy rounds it off with Puglia and its food. Across those three you get everything Turkey offers, water park included, without leaving western Europe.

Below, eight scored all-inclusive addresses in Greece, Spain and Italy, not a single Turkish resort among them. Start with the map, then open the ones that suit your dates.
