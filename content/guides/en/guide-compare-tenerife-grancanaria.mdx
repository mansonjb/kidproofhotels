The Canaries derby. Both big islands share the same base: year-round sun, big resorts, water parks, southern beaches within reach of the airport. The difference is in the detail, and in what you want from a week.

Tenerife is the south (Costa Adeje, Los Cristianos): the biggest resorts, the best clubs, more restaurant choice, and Mount Teide for the big day out. It is the comfort island, a little pricier, a little livelier.

Gran Canaria plays variety and value. Maspalomas and its dunes, Playa del Ingles, Puerto de Mogan, and plenty of aparthotels and self-catering bungalows, ideal when you travel with a baby and want a kitchen of your own. Often a notch cheaper than Tenerife.

Our verdict: comfort and big resorts, Tenerife; variety, value and self-catering, Gran Canaria. Four scored addresses per island, below.
