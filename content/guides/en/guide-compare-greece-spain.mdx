The two giants of the European family holiday, and a genuine question. Greece has exploded in recent years, Spain remains the old reliable. Splitting them mostly comes down to season, flight and the kind of trip.

Greece has the best all-inclusive going right now. The Ikos resorts redefined the genre, a la carte dining included, proper clubs, sandy shallow beaches on Halkidiki, at Kos or Corfu. The trade-off: it is further (three hours plus from northern Europe), and the season runs roughly April to October.

Spain wins on flexibility and winter. The Canaries run all year, the Balearics are two hours away, and supply is so deep you find every price. For guaranteed sun outside summer, or a short flight with a baby, Spain has no rival.

Our verdict: premium summer all-inclusive, Greece; year-round sun, a short flight and a broad budget, Spain. Eight scored resorts, four each, below.
