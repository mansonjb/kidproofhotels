Mallorca is the family classic for a reason: a two-hour hop from much of Europe, a north coast of shallow sandy bays built for children, and a density of good family resorts that keeps prices competitive. If you want a low-stress week that just works, it is hard to beat for 2027.

## What makes Mallorca work with kids

- **The Alcudia and Playa de Muro bays**, long, flat and sandy, the best toddler beaches in the western Med.
- **A short transfer** from Palma to most family bases, so the day you arrive is not written off.
- **Every level of resort**, from big all-inclusives with water slides to calmer aparthotels with a kitchen.
- **Easy days out**, from the train to Soller to gentle boat trips, when the pool needs a break.

## Where to base yourself

Alcudia and Playa de Muro in the north are the family heartland: shallow sea, sand, and resorts built around it. Palmanova and Santa Ponsa in the southwest suit families who want more nearby. Avoid the party strips if a quiet evening matters.
