Mallorca is the classic you almost hesitate to recommend for fear of stating the obvious. And yet. Two hours in the air, a north coast of flat sandy bays built for children, and such a density of good resorts that prices stay sensible. For a week that just works, without the fuss, it is hard to beat.

The north, around Alcudia and Playa de Muro, is the family heartland: a sea you can stand up in for dozens of metres, sand as far as you can see, and hotels built around exactly that. Palmanova and Santa Ponsa in the southwest suit families who like a bit of life nearby. One rule: steer clear of the party strips if your evenings matter to you.

What we love here is that you can get out without it turning into an expedition. The little train to Soller, a boat trip, a cove for a morning: enough to break the pool routine without wearing anyone out.
