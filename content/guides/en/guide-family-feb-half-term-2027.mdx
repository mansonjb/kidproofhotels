February half-term is the hardest week of the year to get right: one precious school break, in the depth of winter, with everyone chasing the same warm-weather flights. Get it wrong and you have paid peak prices for a grey week. Here are eight resorts that deliver genuine, near-guaranteed sun for the February 2027 break, scored for families and mapped.

## The February rule: prioritise certainty

A single week has no room for a gamble on the weather. That pushes the choice towards the surest bets:

- **The Canary Islands** for a four-hour flight and dependable warmth.
- **The Red Sea, the Gulf and Cape Verde** if you will trade a longer flight for a higher, more certain temperature.
- **A heated pool is non-negotiable** in February, even in the Canaries.

## What makes a half-term resort work

- **Everything on site**, because a week is too short to spend hunting for a kid-friendly dinner.
- **A free kids club and enough indoor space** for the odd cooler day.
- **A short transfer**, so the first and last days are not written off.
