February half-term is the one winter week when a family can trade a grey fortnight for real warmth, and the honest answer is to book somewhere the sun is a near-certainty rather than a hope. The Canary Islands are the shortest reliable option from western Europe, roughly a four to four and a half hour flight, with daytime highs around 21 to 23 degrees. If you want the thermometer higher still, the Red Sea, the Gulf, Cape Verde, Morocco and Tunisia all deliver, at the cost of a longer trip or a slightly further reach. One week does not forgive a gamble, so certainty is the whole game.

## Where is the sun most reliable in February?

For a single school week, dependability beats everything. The Canaries (Tenerife, Gran Canaria, Fuerteventura, Lanzarote) are the safe default: warm, dry, and close. The Red Sea (Sharm, Hurghada) and the Gulf (Dubai, Ras al Khaimah) run warmer, often 24 to 26 degrees, with calm, swimmable seas. Cape Verde sits reliably in the mid-20s with an Atlantic breeze. Morocco (Agadir, Marrakech) and Tunisia (Djerba) are pleasant by day but cooler in the evenings, and the sea is bracing. Further out, Mauritius offers full tropical heat if you have the time and budget for a long-haul week.

## Canaries or a longer flight to the Red Sea and Gulf?

This is the core February trade-off. The Canaries win on flight time and no jet lag, which matters enormously with a toddler over a seven-day week. The Red Sea and Gulf win on raw heat and warmer water for swimming.

| | Canaries | Red Sea / Gulf |
|---|---|---|
| Flight from western Europe | ~4 to 4.5 hrs | ~5 to 7 hrs |
| Typical daytime high | 21 to 23 C | 24 to 26 C |
| Sea for swimming | Cool but OK | Warm |
| Jet lag | None | Minimal (1 to 3 hrs) |

If your children are very young, the Canaries usually make the better week. If you have strong swimmers and want guaranteed heat, the longer flight pays off.

## What kind of resort works best for one short week?

A single week is too short to lose a day hunting for a child-friendly dinner, so look for everything on site. A genuine free kids club, at least one heated or indoor pool for the cooler mornings, a short airport transfer, and an all-inclusive or half-board plan so the evenings run themselves. On the Atlantic islands and coasts, wind can pick up in the afternoon, so a resort with a sheltered pool area earns its keep.

## What budget should I plan for February 2027?

February half-term is one of the most in-demand family weeks of the winter, so prices sit well above the November or January shoulder. Expect budget all-inclusive on the Canaries or in Tunisia to start lower, mid-range Canaries and Red Sea resorts in the middle, and Gulf, Tenerife luxury or Mauritius at the top end. The single biggest saver is booking early: fares and the best family rooms climb steadily as the week approaches.

## What ages does a February sun week suit?

It suits every age, but the choice shifts. Babies and toddlers do best on the Canaries, where the short flight and no jet lag protect naps and bedtimes. Primary-age children who swim happily gain the most from the warmer Red Sea and Gulf water. Teens tend to want a resort with more to do: water sports, a bigger pool complex, and a livelier evening.

## FAQ

**When should I book February half-term 2027?**
As early as you can, ideally by autumn 2026. This is a fixed, high-demand week, and both flights and the best family rooms sell out or climb in price the closer you get.

**Is the sea warm enough to swim in February?**
In the Canaries and Morocco the sea is cool, better for a quick dip than long swims, so a heated pool matters. The Red Sea, Gulf and Mauritius stay warm enough for proper swimming.

**Do we need long-haul, or is short-haul enough?**
Short-haul to the Canaries is enough for reliable warmth and is far easier with young children. Long-haul (Mauritius, the Gulf) is worth it mainly for guaranteed heat or if you have longer than a week.

**What is the warmest short-haul option?**
The Red Sea and the Gulf, at around 24 to 26 degrees, are the warmest destinations reachable in well under seven hours from western Europe.
