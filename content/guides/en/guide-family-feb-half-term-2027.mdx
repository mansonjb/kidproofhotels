February half-term is the impossible week: one break, in the depth of winter, with everyone chasing the same flights to the sun. Get it wrong and you pay top price for a grey week. So you leave nothing to chance.

The February rule comes down to one word: certainty. A single week does not forgive a gamble on the weather. The Canaries first, for a four-hour flight and dependable warmth. The Red Sea, the Gulf, Cape Verde if you trade a longer flight for a higher, surer thermometer.

A good half-term resort has everything on site, because a week is too short to go hunting for a dinner that takes children. A free club, something to do indoors on the cooler days, and a short transfer so the first and last days are not written off.
