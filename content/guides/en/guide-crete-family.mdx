Crete has something the small islands do not: room to breathe. You can spend a week without ever leaving the resort, or turn it into a proper adventure, the pink sand at Elafonisi, fishing villages, tavernas at the water's edge, without the children ever losing interest. It is one of the easiest Greek islands for a first family trip.

The north coast holds most of it: long sandy beaches around Chania and Rethymno, made for the age when paddling is the whole point. That is where you find the big resorts with a water park and a kids club, alongside smaller family villages for anyone after a quieter week. One underrated plus: from Heraklion or Chania, most family bases are a short hop away, so the arrival day is not lost to traffic.

A parent's tip: with toddlers, choose the beach on its sand and its gentle slope, not on the postcard. A dramatic but pebbly cove looks lovely in photos and is a pain in real life.
