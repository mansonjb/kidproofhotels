Crete is the island that has everything a family needs and enough space to spread it out: long sandy beaches on the north coast, big resorts with every facility, quieter bays in the west, and a real place to explore when the pool palls. It is one of the easiest first-time family islands in Greece, and 2027 is a good year to book it early.

## What makes Crete work with kids

- **Shallow, sandy beaches** around Chania and Rethymno, ideal for paddling ages.
- **Big all-inclusive resorts** with water parks and free kids clubs, plus smaller family villages for a calmer week.
- **A short north-coast transfer** from Heraklion or Chania airports to most family bases.
- **Something beyond the resort**, from the pink sand at Elafonisi to easy harbour towns, for the days you want out.

## Where to base yourself

The Chania and Rethymno coasts are the sweet spot for scored family resorts. Head towards Elounda in the east for the quieter, higher-end end of the market. Families with toddlers should prioritise a sandy shallow beach over a dramatic one.
