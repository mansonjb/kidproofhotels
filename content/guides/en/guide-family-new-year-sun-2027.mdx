There is a particular kind of relief in spending the festive week somewhere warm: no cold-weather logistics, no cabin fever, just a pool and a beach while home is dark and grey. Christmas and New Year in the sun is one of the most-booked family weeks of the year, and one of the first to sell out. Here are eight resorts that do it well for 2027, scored and mapped.

## Where a warm festive week actually works

- **The Canary Islands** are the family default: four hours, dependable warmth, and resorts that dress the week up with kids clubs and festive dinners.
- **The Red Sea and the Gulf** turn up the heat for a longer flight.
- **Cape Verde** sits in between, warm and low-key.

## What matters over the festive week

- **A heated pool**, because even Canary evenings cool off in late December.
- **A proper festive programme**, if a big Christmas dinner and New Year for the children is the point.
- **Family rooms that sleep everyone**, since this is a week you want to be together and comfortable.
