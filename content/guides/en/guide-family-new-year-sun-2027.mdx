There is a particular relief in spending the festive week somewhere warm: no cold-weather logistics, no cabin fever, just a pool and a beach while it goes dark at five back home. Christmas and New Year in the sun is one of the most-booked family weeks of the year, and one of the first to sell out.

The Canaries are the default: a four-hour flight, dependable warmth, and resorts that dress the week up with kids clubs and festive dinners. The Red Sea and the Gulf turn up the heat for a longer flight. Cape Verde, in between, stays warm and low-key. What matters over the festive week? A proper programme, if a big Christmas and a New Year for the children is the point, and family rooms where you all fit, because this is a week you want to be together and comfortable.
