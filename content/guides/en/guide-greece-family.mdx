A few years ago you sent families to Spain without a second thought. These days Greece has taken the lead, and once you have taken children there, it is easy to see why. The sea is warm and gentle, the flights are short, and a handful of resorts have decided to treat families as the point rather than a complication to manage.

The Ikos properties did a lot of that. Included a la carte dining, proper kids clubs, beaches built for the bucket-and-spade age: they reset what all-inclusive could be. But they are not the only ones, and depending on your children, they may not even be the ones you want.

For an easy first trip, aim for Halkidiki or Kos: big shallow bays, sand, and enough to fill the days without ever getting in a car. Corfu and Zakynthos play a greener, softer hand, ideal when you want to slow right down. And if your kids will spend all day on a water slide given half a chance, steer towards the resorts that make that their thing.
