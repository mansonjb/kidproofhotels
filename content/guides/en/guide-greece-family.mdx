Greece has quietly become the best family beach destination in Europe, and 2027 is the year to book it early. Warm shallow seas, short flights, and a run of resorts that treat families as the main event rather than an afterthought. The Ikos properties rewrote the all-inclusive rulebook here, but they are not the only ones worth your money.

## What makes Greece work with kids

- **Calm, shallow bays** on Halkidiki, Corfu and Kos, so paddling toddlers are safe and swimming children are happy.
- **Genuinely free kids clubs** at the better resorts, not a token hour a day.
- **Food that suits early eaters**, with a la carte dinners included at the top places.
- **Short-ish transfers** from the island airports, which matters more than any brochure admits when you land with tired children.

## Where to base yourself

Halkidiki and Kos are the current benchmarks for scored family resorts, with Corfu and Zakynthos close behind for a greener, quieter week. If your children are at the water-slide age, lean island; if you want big buffets and multiple pools, the Halkidiki mainland resorts deliver.

## Booking 2027

The strongest Greek resorts fill their July and August 2027 weeks from autumn 2026, and the early-booking discounts, often 20 to 30 percent, go first. Hold a refundable room now and watch the price rather than gambling on a late deal that rarely lands on the good ones.
