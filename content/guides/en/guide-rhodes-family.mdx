Rhodes has something few islands can claim: it keeps everyone busy. The little ones get their sandy beaches, the older kids get a medieval old town that turns a rainy afternoon into the best bit of the trip, and the season runs so long you can still swim over October half-term. It is an easy, reassuring pick for 2027.

The southeast coast holds the good family addresses: around Kiotari and Lardos the sea is calm, the sand shows up, and the big water-park resorts compete for your booking. Faliraki suits families who like a bit of buzz within reach. Lindos you have to earn: more charm, fewer facilities, but what a setting.

A word for parents of toddlers: aim for a beach that shelves gently, not a bay where the wave breaks at your feet. The difference between a child happily paddling and a child in tears is sometimes three metres of sand.
