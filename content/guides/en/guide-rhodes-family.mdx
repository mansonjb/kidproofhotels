Rhodes packs a lot of family holiday into one island: reliable sunshine deep into the shoulder season, a long east coast lined with sandy family beaches, and a medieval old town that turns a rainy afternoon into the best day of the trip. It is a strong, easy pick for 2027, and the good resorts book up early.

## What makes Rhodes work with kids

- **Sandy, shallow east-coast beaches** around Kiotari, Lardos and Kolymbia, made for paddling and sandcastles.
- **Big family resorts with water parks** alongside smaller, calmer options near Lindos.
- **A genuine day out** in Rhodes old town and at Lindos, so the week is not only pool and beach.
- **A long season**, with warm sea well into October for half-term escapes.

## Where to base yourself

The southeast coast around Kiotari and Lardos is the family heartland, with the biggest scored resorts and the calmest sea. Faliraki suits families who want more around them; Lindos rewards those after charm over facilities.
