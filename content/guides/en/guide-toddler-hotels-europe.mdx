Travelling with a one to four year old is a different holiday, and the resort makes or breaks it. The right one has a shallow pool you can stand in, a beach without a shore break, a room that gets a toddler to sleep by seven, and food they will actually eat. Here are eight European resorts that genuinely deliver that, scored and mapped for 2027.

## What a toddler resort has to get right

- **A shallow pool with a separate splash area**, warm and gently sloping, not one deep tank.
- **A calm, sandy beach** with no sudden drop, so paddling is relaxing rather than white-knuckle.
- **A real family room or suite**, ideally with a separate sleeping space so the evening is yours once they are down.
- **Early dinner and toddler-friendly food**, plus the kit that saves your luggage: cots, high chairs, a bottle warmer.

## Where to look

Shallow-bay destinations win: the Alcudia coast on Mallorca, the calmer Greek islands, the Canaries for winter sun. A free kids club matters less at this age than the pool, the beach and the room, so weight those.

## Booking 2027

The resorts that suit toddlers are the ones parents rebook, so 2027 peak weeks go early. Hold a refundable room now, compare on the map, and prioritise the beach and pool over the brochure gloss. The eight below are where we would start.
