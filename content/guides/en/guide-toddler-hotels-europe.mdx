Travelling with a one to four year old is a different holiday. The resort does not just matter, it decides everything. The right one has a pool you can stand up in, a beach with no wave breaking at the shore, a room that gets the little one down by seven, and food they will eat without a scene. The wrong one turns the week into a marathon.

What a toddler resort has to get right comes down to a few things. A shallow, warm pool with a separate splash area, not one deep tank. A calm, sandy beach that shelves gently. A real family room or suite, ideally with a separate sleeping space, so the evening is yours once they are asleep. And the kit that saves your luggage: a cot, a high chair, a bottle warmer.

At this age the kids club matters less than the pool, the beach and the room. Those three are what we weigh. Shallow-bay destinations win hands down: the Alcudia coast on Mallorca, the calmer Greek islands, the Canaries for winter sun.
