October half-term is the quiet gem of the calendar: the crowds have gone, the prices have dropped, and much of the Mediterranean is still warm enough to swim. The catch is that the map has shrunk since summer. You need to know which coasts still hold up in late October.

Rhodes, Cyprus and southern Turkey keep their heat the longest, with a sea still pleasant to the end of the month. The Canaries step in as the reliable fallback, warm all year. Crete and Malta suit the earlier part of the break. A good late-season resort heats its pools for the cooler evenings and keeps its kids club in full swing, where others wind down after summer.

October's real draw is the price: one of the best warm weeks of the year, for far less than July.
