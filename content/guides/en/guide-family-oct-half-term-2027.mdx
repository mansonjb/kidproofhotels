For October half-term 2027, the warmest reliable family sun is in the far south and east of the Mediterranean, Rhodes, Cyprus, southern Turkey and Malta, with the Canary Islands as the year-round fallback. The crowds and prices have dropped since summer, but the map has shrunk too: by late October you need coasts that still hold their heat, and a resort that heats its pools and keeps the kids club running.

## Where is it still warm enough to swim in late October?

The south-eastern Med keeps its warmth longest. Rhodes, Cyprus and the Turkish Riviera around Antalya and Belek stay pleasant to the end of the month, with a sea often still 22 to 24C. Crete and Malta suit the earlier days of the break better than the last. Madeira sits mild and green all autumn, though its Atlantic beaches are more about pools than long swims. And when you want a guarantee, the Canary Islands (Gran Canaria, Tenerife) are warm every month of the year.

Flights from western Europe are manageable: roughly 3 to 4 hours to Rhodes, Cyprus and southern Turkey, about 4 hours to the Canaries and Madeira, and around 3 hours to Malta.

## Which destination is the safest bet for guaranteed sun?

If you cannot risk a grey week, the Canaries are the answer: Atlantic-warmed, dependable, and warm into the evening. The south-eastern Med is warmer still when it behaves, and usually does in October, but it carries a little more weather risk late in the month. Madeira is the mild middle ground, lovely for walking and pools rather than a beach holiday.

## Which resort suits our children's ages?

- **Toddlers (1 to 4):** a heated pool close to the room matters most now, because the sea can feel cool by late October even where the air is warm. Calm Canarian and Cypriot resorts work well.
- **Kids (5 to 11):** a heated pool complex and an all-day club keep them busy when the sea is borderline; big Rhodes and Turkey resorts deliver.
- **Teens:** they will swim regardless, so lean towards the warmest water, southern Turkey, Rhodes or Cyprus, plus somewhere with a bit of life after dinner.

## What should a good late-season resort do?

The season is the test. A great October resort heats its pools for the cooler evenings and keeps its kids club and entertainment in full swing, where lesser resorts wind down after summer. Ask before you book: are the pools heated, is the club running at half-term dates, and is the main restaurant still on full service? Sun beds and shade matter less now; a warm indoor or covered space for a rare wet afternoon matters more.

## Is October half-term cheaper than summer?

Yes, and that is its real draw. You get one of the last genuinely warm weeks of the year for far less than July or August. All-inclusive still suits the Canaries and Turkey, where staying on-site is easy; half-board works on Rhodes or in Malta if you want to eat out. Budget resorts sit at the lower per-night bands, family clubs in the middle, and the polished names at the top, all softer than peak summer.

## FAQ

**Can you still swim in the sea at October half-term?** Yes, in the south-eastern Med (Rhodes, Cyprus, southern Turkey) and the Canaries, where the water is usually 22 to 24C. Crete and Malta cool sooner, so favour a heated pool there.

**Where is guaranteed sun in late October?** The Canary Islands are the safest bet, warm every month; the eastern Med is warmer but carries a slightly higher weather risk late in the month.

**Do we need a resort with heated pools?** For toddlers and younger kids, yes. Evenings cool off even where days are warm, and a heated pool saves the holiday when the sea feels chilly.

**Is it much cheaper than summer?** Noticeably. Prices drop well below July and August across all these regions, which is a big part of why half-term is such good value.

**How far ahead should we book?** Half-term is popular precisely because it is warm, quiet and affordable, so the best family resorts still sell out. Hold a refundable room a few months ahead and compare.
