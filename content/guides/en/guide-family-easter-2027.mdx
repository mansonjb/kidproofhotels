Easter is the first real chance of the year for family warmth without summer prices, but in early April the Mediterranean is uneven: some coasts are already swimmable while others sit at 18 degrees with a sea that still bites. The short answer is to choose your latitude carefully. The Canary Islands are the safest bet for both warm days and swimmable water; the southern and eastern Med (Cyprus, southern Turkey, Crete, the Costa del Sol, Malta) is warm on land but the sea is a coin toss. Pick the right place and Easter gives you more resort for your money than any week until autumn.

## Where is it warm enough to swim by Easter?

The Canaries are the only near-guarantee: daytime highs around 22 to 24 degrees and a sea just about warm enough for children who do not mind a brisk start. On the mainland and larger islands, the air is pleasant (often 20 to 23 degrees on the Costa del Sol, Cyprus, Crete and Malta) but the sea is typically 17 to 19 degrees, fine for paddling, cold for real swimming. Antalya and southern Turkey warm up early too, though the water is still on the cool side. The rule of thumb: for guaranteed sea swimming, go Atlantic and south; everywhere else, plan for a heated pool.

## Canaries or the southern Mediterranean?

This is the Easter decision. The Canaries win on certainty and swimmable water. The southern Med wins on shorter flights from much of Europe, lower prices, and the classic Mediterranean feel.

| | Canaries | Southern Med |
|---|---|---|
| Daytime high | 22 to 24 C | 20 to 23 C |
| Sea for swimming | Just swimmable | Cool (17 to 19 C) |
| Flight from western Europe | ~4 to 4.5 hrs | ~2.5 to 4 hrs |
| Price at Easter | Moderate to high | Often better value |

If your family must swim in the sea, the Canaries are the safer call. If you are happy with a heated pool and want shorter flights and better prices, the southern Med delivers.

## What kind of resort works best at Easter?

Because the weather can still turn, look for a resort that works rain or shine. A heated or indoor pool is the single most useful feature at this time of year. Add a kids club to fill a cooler afternoon, indoor play space, and enough on site that a grey day does not derail the week. All-inclusive or half-board takes the pressure off the evenings, which are still cool enough that dinner is usually indoors.

## Why is Easter better value than summer?

Easter falls before the July and August peak, so the same resort often costs noticeably less than in high summer, and the beaches and pools are far quieter. You trade the guarantee of hot sea for lower prices and space to breathe, which for families with young children is frequently the better deal. It is also one of the few warm weeks that does not require booking a year ahead, though the best resorts still go early.

## What ages does an Easter holiday suit?

It suits all ages with the right expectations. Babies and toddlers do well anywhere with a heated pool and a short transfer, and benefit from the quieter, cooler conditions. Primary-age children who want to swim in the sea are happiest in the Canaries. Teens and confident swimmers can handle the cooler Mediterranean water, especially in the warmer south and east of the basin.

## FAQ

**Can we swim in the sea at Easter?**
In the Canaries, usually yes, if your children do not mind cool water. Across the wider Mediterranean the sea is generally too cold for comfortable swimming in early April, so choose a resort with a heated pool.

**Is Easter cheaper than summer?**
Yes. Easter falls before the summer peak, so resorts are typically better value and far less crowded than in July and August.

**Where is warmest at Easter with a short flight?**
The Costa del Sol, southern Cyprus, Crete, Malta and southern Turkey are the warmest short-haul options on land, though the sea is still cool. For warmth plus swimmable water, the Canaries are worth the slightly longer flight.

**Do we still need to book early?**
Easter is a fixed, in-demand week, so the best family resorts and fares do sell out. You do not need to book a year ahead, but a few months of lead time protects both price and choice.
