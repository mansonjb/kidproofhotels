Easter is the first real chance of the year for a warm family week without the peak-summer prices, but the Mediterranean is uneven in early April. Some coasts are already swimmable, others are a fresh 18 degrees with a cool sea. Here are eight family resorts that genuinely deliver by Easter 2027, scored and mapped so you can match the week to the weather.

## Where Easter is warm enough

- **The Canary Islands** are the safe bet, warm and swimmable while mainland Europe is still waking up.
- **The southern and eastern Med** come good early: Cyprus, southern Turkey, Crete and the Costa del Sol are usually pleasant by the school holidays, warm on land if not always for long swims.
- **A heated pool still helps**, because an April sea can be bracing even under blue skies.

## What to look for

- **A resort that works rain or shine**, with indoor play and a covered pool for a cooler afternoon.
- **A free kids club** to fill the gaps if the beach is a day or two off.
- **Value**, because Easter sits before the summer price jump. This is the week to get more resort for your money.
