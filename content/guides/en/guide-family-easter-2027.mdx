Easter is the first real window of the year for family warmth without the summer prices. The catch is that in early April the Mediterranean is temperamental: some coasts are already swimmable, others sit at 18 degrees with a sea that bites. It is all about landing in the right place.

The safest bet is the Canaries, warm and swimmable while the rest of Europe is barely awake. The south and east of the basin, Cyprus, southern Turkey, Crete, the Costa del Sol, warm up early too, pleasant on land by the holidays even if the swimming still has to be earned. What you want is a resort that works rain or shine: indoor play, a club to fill in if the beach is a day or two off.

And then there is the price. Easter falls before the summer jump, so it is the week you get more resort for your money. A shame to pass it up.
